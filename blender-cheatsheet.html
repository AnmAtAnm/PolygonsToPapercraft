Blender keyboard shortcuts and viewport navigation

3D Navigation via Gizmo

Click or drag the gizmo
  • Axis label: jump to that orthographic view.
  • Matching empty circle: jump to the opposite view.
  • Hover near the gizmo to reveal a circle; drag inside it to orbit.
Use the four tool icons
  • Magnifying glass: drag up/down to zoom.
  • Hand: drag to pan up, down, left, or right.
  • Camera: toggle camera view.
  • Grid: toggle orthographic / perspective.

Numberpad corner keys jump to fixed views or their opposite. The even numbers orbit the view in that cardinal direction.

+ zooms in.

− zooms out.

Navigating with the middle mouse button

  • OrbitDrag with the middle mouse button to rotate the camera around your scene.
  • PanHold Shift and drag with the middle mouse button to slide the view.
Tip: If you lose your object, select it in the Outliner, click back in the viewport for focus, and press . on the numberpad to bring it back into view.

Simple workflow

repeat this loop
  1. Select your target (objects in Layout; vertex/edge/face when Modeling).
    Use Shift to add to existing selection.
  2. Select action/transform type (Grab/Rotate/Scale)
  3. Optional: Constrain X/Y/Z axis
  4. Move mouse (no mouse button) for amount and direction (no mouse button), using Shift for precision or Ctrl for increments,
    or type a numeric value.
  5. Enter to commit, or ESC to cancel & revert

Transform selected

making changes

When an object, face, edge, or vertex is selected, use these commands for basic changes.

G

Grab / move

Press G to move. Add X, Y, or Z to stay on one axis.

G+X→move along X

Shift makes smaller movements.

R

Rotate

Press R to rotate. Add X, Y, or Z to rotate around that axis.

R+X→rotate around XR+R→free rotate
S

Scale

Press S to make the object larger or smaller. Add X, Y, or Z to stretch on one axis.

S+X→stretch along X
Finish or undo: Enter commits a move, rotation, or scale. Esc backs out and reverts it.

Undo or adjust

correcting mistakes
  • Ctrl+ZUndo — reverse the last action.
  • Shift+Ctrl+ZRedo — restore the action.
  • F9Adjust last operation — reopen the last operation's settings.
Remember: red = X, green = Y, blue = Z. The colored gizmo labels match these axes.

Modeling mode

top-row numbers

In Edit Mode, the top-row number keys choose what part of a mesh you select:

1

Points / vertices

2

Edges

3

Faces

Easy to mix up: numberpad 1 is a front view; top-row 1 is vertex selection in Edit Mode.

Viewport controls

panels + tools
  • NSidebar on/off — the right-side panel for numerical tweaks to your most recent change.
  • TToolbar on/off — the tool shelf on the left side of the viewport.
  • Shift+AAdd an object — opens the Add menu.
